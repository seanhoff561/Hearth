# Flora

*Status: planned (V2-6). Species data started in V2-0 (`data/hearth/flora`).*

## Planned model
Species with growth form, climate envelope, soil and light preferences, lifecycle and phenology,
dispersal, growth rate, real maximum sizes, wood material, usable parts by season with
edibility, flammability and appearance. Trees grow procedurally by species (crown shape,
branching, taper), with log blocks for trunks and sub-block branch models; foliage is passable
and casts partial shade; trees are felled as a physical event. Succession and wildfire run on
ecological cells; LOD shows the vegetation state.
