# Inventory, carrying and clothing

*Status: planned (V2-4). Items and garments already in data (`items/`, `clothing/`).*

## Planned model
Every item has mass, volume and a grid footprint (from its form and material). Things go in
hands (one each, or both for large loads), a loincloth tie, clothing attachment points and
containers with their own grids and limits; containers nest. Loads above ~25% of body mass
slow and tire; above ~50% only short lifts, dragging or rolling. Quick slots 1–6 map to belt and
attachment points plus a radial wheel. Clothing layers add insulation, wind/water resistance,
capacity and noise.
