# The Observer and the player among people (H9)

*V2.1 §15.4 and §16; Addendum B §2; D208–D210.*

## Watching the world

The Observer is a way of watching humanity's world without living in it, from the main menu
(**Watch this world**, any world, an ended one too), from the pause menu (**Watch the world**:
the player put aside) and after death (**Watch the world** on the death screen, the choices a
press of Esc away).

- **Put aside.** A living player who watches is put aside where it stands: its body still, its
  needs held, harmed by nothing; the world goes on without it. **Esc** steps back in.
- **The eye.** A free camera. The world streams and lives about the eye, not the player: the
  bands near it are lived in full, the animals about it, and nothing perceives it (no animal, no
  person; the player's band does not see it).
- **Following.** **E** follows the person (else the animal) the eye looks at, the camera after
  them; a person's life is read at the side: who they are, their parents, their partner, and what
  befell them — pairing, children, things found out, camps moved, fights, being taken in or cast
  out — in years of the world's calendar.
- **Time.** **[** and **]**: stopped, as lived, a minute, an hour, a day, a month, a year, ten or a
  hundred years a second. At a month a second and faster the globe opens, as the spec has it:
  the view the map, the people lived as households and numbers.
- **The chronicle.** **J**: the living world's notable events (a band's camp moved, a band split,
  a technique found out, a stranger taken in, one cast out, a fight) newest first, then deep
  time's (a species appearing, reaching a realm, crossing a sea, a technique first known or lost,
  a people parting from another, two species meeting, the last of one). Each is a place: chosen,
  the eye goes there.
- **Overlays.** On the globe (**M**), **1** where people live (thin to thick), **2** their peoples —
  each culture and its language a colour — **3** where a technique is known (again for the next),
  **4** how the peoples look (their gene pools' sunlight, paler to darker), **5** none. Drawn from
  deep time's demes as they stand and the bands lived about the eye.
- **Stepping in.** Alive: back into one's life. Dead: the choices — live on as another, be born
  again, begin again (Addendum B §2).

## The player among people

- **Looking at a person** within eight metres tells what the player's person knows of them and
  no more: their name once learned (kin and band-mates are known by name; others are "a woman", "a
  man", "a girl", "a boy" until told), their kinship or band, whether they are paired, how they
  seem to take the player (fond, at ease, wary, at odds), the ledger between them (who owes whom),
  what the player has heard of them (generous, stingy, a thief). Speakers in the subtitles are
  named the same way.
- **The talk wheel** (hold **K** toward one, lean the mouse, let go): greet, tell one's name,
  thank, praise, joke, apologise, ask to be shown how, offer to show them, ask to stay with them,
  ask to pair, beckon, embrace, threaten, insult. Answers come back in words, and the person says
  and gestures what it would.
- **Being taught** (V2.1 §11.2). Asked, one of the player's band — or one who trusts it — shows it
  something it knows that the player does not, twice as fast as being shown at another's work,
  for about an hour and a half while the player keeps within three metres.
- **Teaching.** Offered, a band-mate, the player's child or one who trusts it learns from the
  player the same way.
- **Joining a group.** Asking to stay makes the player a band's guest when the one asked trusts it;
  the band takes it in as it takes in any guest, once most of its grown trust it (H4).
- **A family.** Pairing is mutual only: a grown, unpaired person of the player's band, not its
  kin, fond of the player and trusting it, is willing; anyone else says no. A paired player's
  person has children as anyone does (abstracted), who grow up in the band; after death the
  player may live on as one of them, grown (Addendum B §2).

## Life after death in full (D208)

**Knowledge after death** — theirs only, a head start, keep everything — and **being born again**
are world settings beside the scope of whom one may live on as; v2's rules are the presets
Legacy, Hardy and Permadeath beside Authentic, the default.
