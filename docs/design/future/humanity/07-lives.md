# 7. Lives among people: births, childhood, any station in life

*Amendment E §10.7. Safety rules in `10-safety.md`.*

## Being born
Choose an era → the History Engine runs to its date → choose a place → choose a **birth from
the real households of any station that exists there**: a ruler's heir, a farmer's child, a
herder's, a smith's, a servant's, a wandering family's. Each household is described soberly
and truthfully: who they are, how they live, what they own and owe.

## Childhood is your character's past
The present runs in real time, so a childhood cannot happen in it. It is a sequence of
**vignettes**, real scenes with your actual family as C4 minds:
- Your parents talk to you and **teach you** (real knowledge transfer into your
  `KnowledgeState`) and keep you close.
- Wander off, and they call, search, grab you and carry you back, scold you and worry. You can
  get lost, scraped and frightened.
- In rare, genuinely dangerous situations (a river, a cliff edge, a predator) a vignette can end
  in the child's death. Such a moment **cuts away before any harm is shown** and is never
  graphic; the family's grief is real.
- **No one can ever deliberately harm a child** (`10-safety.md` rule 2).
- Years pass between vignettes as history; your choices in them shape your skills,
  relationships and memories.
- When you come of age, **the present begins**.

## Living
Work, status, friendships and enmities, marriage, children born in real time, politics and
war, with C2–C4 minds about you.

## Death in an era with people
"Live on as another person" (V2.1 Addendum B) returns: another member of the household or
community, or a birth again, in eras that have people. Wild Earth keeps E §6.6's new adult.
