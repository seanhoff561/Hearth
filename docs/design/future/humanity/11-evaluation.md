# 11. Evaluation

*Amendment E §10.11.*

| What | How | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Believability** | blind comparisons of people's behaviour against alternatives and recorded human play; rubric-based judging (does the person act from their goals, remember, respond to what happened); playtests | F0's prototype, each mind milestone (F4–F7) |
| **Era plausibility** | reviews per era by its sources (archaeology and history of the period): subsistence, settlement, dress, tools, social forms | each era (F11) |
| **Consistency** | every chronicle claim checked against the event store; minds' beliefs against what their person could know; contradiction rates tracked | F3 onward |
| **Social-science sanity** | demography (life expectancy, fertility, age pyramids against the period's estimates), economics (price behaviour, trade volumes), conflict rates (deaths in war per century against the range of estimates for comparable societies) | F2, F8 |
| **Long-run stability** | soak runs of centuries: no unexplained population explosions or collapses, no runaway prices, polities that rise and fall at plausible rates | F2, F12 (the soak suite) |
| **Performance and cost** | frame and tick budgets with N people at each level; inference cost per hour of play per Minds setting | every Phase F audit |
| **Safety** | red-team suites for every rule of `10-safety.md`; must always pass | CI from F1 |

Results go into each Phase F audit's report (an audit after every three milestones).
