# Playtest issues (Amendments P and E)

The owner's playtest reports and Amendment P's findings (`docs/spec/amendment-p-playability.md`),
one numbered item each: how to reproduce it, its cause once found, its status and the milestone
that fixes it. Kept current through P0–P8; it is how the owner checks the fixes. Causes marked
*(reproduced)* were seen happening, with numbers; *(confirmed by …)* worked out from the code's
own numbers; *(from the code)* are what the game lacks (a screen, a mode, a pose), confirmed
absent in the source, and are built by the milestone named.

Statuses: **open**, **cause found**, **fixed** (with the test or screenshot that shows it),
**explained** (working as designed, with why).

| 6 | Short grass slows running as much as a thicket | Sprint across a steppe or a heath | *(reproduced)* `hearth_physics::mover` slowed a body by the **largest** `drag` of any block its box overlapped, whatever the plant's height and however little of the body it touched: the understory's grasses and dwarf shrubs carry drags of 0.05–0.4 (`blocks/understory.json`), so a sprint through ankle-high steppe fescue (0.4 m, drag 0.1) lost 10 % and through heather (0.5 m, 0.15) 15 %, as at a shoulder-high shrub's edge; tree leaves slowed by 0.42–0.55 at the feet as at the chest | **fixed** (P0): each column of plants the box overlaps slows it by its density × (how high it reaches up the body ÷ the body's height)² × its share of the box's footprint, and a plant stands as tall as its species grows (`max_height_m`). Over the 94 understory plants: fescue 0.5 %, heather 1.2 %, every grass and herb below the knee at most 2 %, knee- to waist-high grasses 2–5 %, shrubs and tall reeds 10–40 % (`plants_slow_a_sprint_by_how_much_of_the_body_they_reach`, `brushing_a_thicket_slows_less_than_pushing_through_it`; `docs/design/movement.md`) | P0 |
| 7 | Clouds race across the sky | Watch the sky for a minute | *(reproduced)* `environment.rs` set the clouds at `days × 86 400 × 0.02 × wind`: the world's whole age times the wind of the moment, so every change of the wind moved them by the age times the change; a world 400 days old moved them 50–390 m a tick (1–8 km/s) as the weather's wind shifted | **fixed** (P0): the drift is summed from frame to frame at the wind at the cloud base (about twice the ten-metre wind) in real seconds, the wind's turn eased over two minutes, a jump in time carrying them two seconds' worth (`clouds_drift_at_the_wind_aloft_in_real_seconds`; `docs/design/motion-timing.md`) | P0 |
| 8 | Stars flash | A clear night | *(reproduced)* not the twinkle (`sin(t · (3 + h · 400))` with `h` below 0.012: under 1.3 flickers a second, ±20 %) but how the stars were drawn: each star a Gaussian of half a pixel at 1080p, cut off at the edge of its cell of the star grid, so as the compressed day turns the sky (some 2 pixels a second at 1080p) a star's light jumped as it crossed pixels and cell edges. Eight frames over 0.8 s at 1080p (`tools/shots/p0_motion.shots`): the stars' light varied by 27–33 % (median), their brightest pixel by 62–72 %, and 28 % of them vanished in at least one frame | **fixed** (P0): each star summed over the 27 cells about the pixel, so no cell's edge cuts it, at its own crisp width (widened only where a pixel is coarser than a star; a first try at half a pixel and more looked blurred, the owner preferred the old shape); scintillation of a few hertz, 1.5 % overhead to 21.5 % at the horizon, halved in calm air. The same frames: light steady to 2–4 %, the brightest pixel varying 36–47 % (a point crossing pixels), 7 % of the faintest dipping below sight for a frame; as many stars as before (`docs/review/p0/stars_flicker.gif`, before left, after right; `motion-timing.md`) | P0 |
