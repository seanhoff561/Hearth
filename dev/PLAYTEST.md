# Playtest issues (Amendments P and E)

The owner's playtest reports and Amendment P's findings (`docs/spec/amendment-p-playability.md`),
one numbered item each: how to reproduce it, its cause once found, its status and the milestone
that fixes it. Kept current through P0–P8; it is how the owner checks the fixes. Causes marked
*(reproduced)* were seen happening, with numbers; *(confirmed by …)* worked out from the code's
own numbers; *(from the code)* are what the game lacks (a screen, a mode, a pose), confirmed
absent in the source, and are built by the milestone named.

Statuses: **open**, **cause found**, **fixed** (with the test or screenshot that shows it),
**explained** (working as designed, with why).
